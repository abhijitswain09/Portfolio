01 / About

Science-minded.
Engineering-focused.

At Genomiki Solutions, I work across diagnostic NGS analysis, clinical variant interpretation, microbial and transcriptomic analysis, and development of bioinformatics products and workflows. I enjoy connecting the analytical details with the bigger clinical or research question.
